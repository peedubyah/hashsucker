Method: curl Range request bytes=N*8388608..(N+1)*8388608-1, SHA-256 of returned body.
Each chunk returned by the P11 demand-path seam is bit-identical to the chunk
returned by the no-prefetch direct path, regardless of whether the demand
read was served from prefetched_done (served_demand), joined to an in-flight
prefetch fill (joined_by_demand), or fetched directly.

P11 demand-path seam returns the same bytes the provider does, in all three
delivery paths.
