# Product Direction — Household Media Continuity

A thesis, not doctrine. Canonical: `GOALS.md` (north star, non-goals).

## The idea

The household owns **intent** (what it wants), **exact representations**
(which bytes satisfy it), and **availability knowledge** (how to play
them right now). Providers, disks, and media servers are interchangeable
routes and views. Replace any of them and nothing is re-curated.

## Where that leads, if evidence earns it

- A library that survives supplier death without user action.
- Next-episode readiness (the one prediction with real base rates).
- Representation choice from observed playback outcomes, not filenames.
- Local retention as keep-policy on routes — cache, retain, or remote-only.
- Prediction only where measured; explicit intent over clever vetoes.

## Non-goals (standing)

- No recommendation engine or taste modeling.
- No native player; no ceremonial Emby support.
- No generic route abstraction without need.
- No AI theater; no tracker identity as the core product.
- No new product surface before the reliability exit condition is met.

## Open research question (not a requirement)

> What is the minimum durable state a household needs to replace every
> provider, disk, and media server without re-curating its media life?
